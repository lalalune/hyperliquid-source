## Evidence

Original addresses (VA) and the coordinate system above. Validated
boundaries: FDE `0x63d8790..0x63d88c4` (308 bytes) from
`.eh_frame`; entry begins with `push %r14` (no `endbr64` here —
the #233 caution: bounds come from `.eh_frame`, not from
scanning for `endbr64`); single `ret` at
`0x63d87f7`; `int3` padding `0x63d88c4..0x63d88d0`; the `nopl`
gaps `0x63d87f8..0x63d8800` and `0x63d88a8..0x63d88b0` are padding,
not claimed.

Raw disassembly: the four exhibit pairs (`hd_entry`, `hd_size`,
`hd_tail`, `hd_over`; 292 bytes, 82 strict mnemonic lines) beside
this record, regenerated byte-for-byte by the verifier.

Callers (direct `E8`, each confirmed by objdump + rel32):
`0x63d9232` (FDE `0x63d9070..0x63d9337`, unclaimed neighbour) and
`0x63d966a` (block register FDE, #237). No `E9`, no rip-relative
`LEA`, no `.rela.dyn` addend equal to `0x63d8790`.

Callees (cited only): `0x63e8bf0` (size mapping, call at
`0x63d8810`), `0x63b9680` (entry probe, call at `0x63d8874`),
`0x63b99d0` (tail jump at `0x63d88a3`, never returns here).

ABI/layout observations: first argument in `rdi` (saved in `rbp`),
block pointer in `rsi` (saved in `rbx`); block links at `+0x28`
and `+0x30`; flag bytes at `+0x11` (gate, read by callers),
`+0x12` (set here), `+0x13` and `+0x14` (read by callers, not
here); dispatch word `[block+0x68]`; size word `[block+0x60]`;
list anchor `[first-arg+0x1080]`; class bitmap base
`first-arg+0x400`; size-class entries are 16 bytes at
`first-arg + 16 * index`; overflow entry `first-arg+0xc700`,
bitmap index 199.

Rounding/overflow observations: the size-class index uses
`bsr` with a power-of-two correction (`cmp`/`sbb`), a minimum
clamp of 14, and a 2-bit extraction from `(result - 1)`; the
overflow threshold is `0x7000000000000000` (strictly greater
branches to the stub).

Analysis was complete over the FDE: every instruction between
`0x63d8790` and `0x63d88c4` is accounted for in the README's
path walk, except the two `nopl` padding gaps (which contain no
instructions).
