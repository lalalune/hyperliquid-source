Relocations for the hash merge-accumulators window (from .rela.dyn and .rela.plt):

the routine 0x61c27f0 itself: no R_X86_64_RELATIVE record carries it as
  addend; no whole-file stored qword equals it

The window is reached only through the two direct calls at
0x61c2c8e and 0x61c31fc (see census.py output, reproduced by
verify.py). The body itself resolves no GOT slot: it makes no
call of any kind, reads no GOT entry, and its only transfers
are the balanced push/pop of %r12 and the final ret. Neither
caller passes this routine's address in memory: both load the
argument registers directly. The seedless caller loads the
secret pointer 0x97df0b with a RIP-relative lea at 0x61c2c80 --
that records the default secret's address, not this routine's.
