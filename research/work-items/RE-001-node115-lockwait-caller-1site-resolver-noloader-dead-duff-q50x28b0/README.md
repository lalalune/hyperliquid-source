## Selector and copy

The copy IS a Duff's-device loop (0x639a588..0x639a6a7), but it is FULLY DEAD. The length is *r8 (`mov 0x0(%r14),%rdx` at 0x639a511, r14 = entry r8); if the length is exactly 8, the qword from the stack temp (%rsp) is stored whole to *rcx (`mov %rsi,(%r12)` at 0x639a51a) and the window exits 0 (`xor %ebp,%ebp` at 0x639a51e). A null dest or null length pointer also exits 0 (`je 0x639a51e` at 0x639a50a / 0x639a50f). Otherwise n = min(len,8) (`mov $0x8,%edi` at 0x639a540, `mov %rsp,%r9` at 0x639a545, `cmp %rdi,%rdx` at 0x639a548, `cmova %rdi,%rdx` at 0x639a54b); len < 8 goes to the small tail (`jb 0x639a6b0` at 0x639a552), which copies n = len bytes by 4/2/1-byte decomposition (0x639a6b0..0x639a6f9) and exits 22 with *r8 = n. The 1-byte path sets ebp = first byte (`movzbl (%rsp),%ebp` at 0x639a6b9) as a transient, but that def is dead — the writeback's `mov $0x16,%ebp` at 0x639a6c9 overwrites it on both paths out (fall-through to 0x639a6c6, or via the 2-byte path at 0x639a6ea then `jmp 0x639a6c6` at 0x639a6f9). Len > 8 reaches the n == 8 path: `mov %edx,%eax` at 0x639a558 sets eax = 8; the first qword is stored to dest+0 (`mov %rsi,(%r12)` at 0x639a55a), `lea 0x8(%r12),%rdi` at 0x639a55e, re-loaded from src+0 (`mov -0x8(%r9,%rax,1),%rcx` at 0x639a563 — with eax == 8 reads src+0, not src+8), `and $~7,%rdi` at 0x639a568, and stored AGAIN to dest+0 (`mov %rcx,-0x8(%r12,%rax,1)` at 0x639a56c), then r12d = (dest - ((dest+8)&~7) + 8) & ~7 = 0 (`sub %rdi,%r12` at 0x639a571, `sub %r12,%r9` at 0x639a574, `add %edx,%r12d` at 0x639a577, `and $~7,%r12d` at 0x639a57a), so `cmp $0x8,%r12d` at 0x639a57e leaves r12d < 8 and the loop-entry jb (`jb 0x639a6c6` at 0x639a582) is ALWAYS taken to the writeback.
