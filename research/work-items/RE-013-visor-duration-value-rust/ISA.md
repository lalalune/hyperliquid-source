# Conditional ISA prerequisites

These primary manuals support generic instruction rules only. They do not supply an independent observation of the Visor program, a measured target CPU/MXCSR, an original compiler/type identity or captured execution. The chosen native domain fixes nearest-even, masks relevant floating exceptions, disables DAZ/FTZ and excludes traps/asynchronous changes.

[Intel SDM Volume1,253665-080US,June2023](https://cdrdv2-public.intel.com/782149/253665-sdm-vol-1.pdf), sections4.8.3.5,4.8.4,4.9.1.4 and Tables4-8/4-10: exact arithmetic is rounded to the specified binary64 result; nearest ties choose an even significand, with MXCSR controlling SSE rounding. Masked positive overflow returns positive infinity. NaN arithmetic preserves nonfinite class, with signaling exceptions handled under the explicit mask premise. The threshold used here is derived mathematically from the largest finite value and the next binade; it is not a second measured result.

[Intel SDM Volume2B,253667-084US,June2024](https://cdrdv2-public.intel.com/825757/253667-sdm-vol-2b.pdf), UCOMISD/MULSD/MINSD/MAXSD entries: unordered comparisons include NaNs; UCOMISD sets ZF/PF/CF to111 for unordered,000 for greater,001 for less and100 for equality. An unmasked signaling exception prevents normal flag update. Legacy MULSD updates the low destination lane. MINSD/MAXSD equal finite values select their second operand, including opposite signed zeros; the proof needs only the resulting numeric zero when this arises.

[Intel SDM Volume2A,253666-093US,September2026](https://cdrdv2.intel.com/v1/dl/getContent/671199), ADDSD/CVTTSD2SI entries: legacy ADDSD adds the two low binary64 values. CVTTSD2SI with REX.W converts to signed64 by truncation; out-of-range conversion raises invalid, and the masked response is the signed indefinite word. The proof explicitly permits this intermediate event on2^64 and shows the later comparison overwrites its value before return. It does not reproduce FP status flags.

The editions are identified, not collectively claimed latest.
