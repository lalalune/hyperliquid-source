Relocation / PLT notes for the RE-001 guard release window (node #115).

Window 0x5f0b5e0..0x5f0b605:
- One call leaves the window: at 0x5f0b600, call to the PLT stub
  at 0x63f0f90. The stub is jmp *0xfd482(%rip) to GOT slot
  0x64ee418, which carries an R_X86_64_JUMP_SLOT relocation for
  _ZSt20__throw_system_errori (std::__throw_system_error(int),
  GLIBCXX_3.4.11, addend 0). The stub pushes index 0x55, matching
  the .rela.plt entry whose r_offset is 0x64ee418.
- The call is the window's throw tail: edi is loaded with the
  constant 1 immediately before it, and the callee is noreturn,
  so control never returns into the window from it.
- No other relocation touches the window: no GOT read, no
  rip-relative operand and no .rodata reference appears in the
  remaining eleven instructions; the only memory operands are
  rdi-relative (the slot) and rax-indirect (the lock byte).
- No whole-file qword hit and no R_X86_64_RELATIVE addend
  resolves to the window (see census.py): it is not a vtable
  slot, not a callback and not stored anywhere as data.
