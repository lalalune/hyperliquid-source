Relocation / PLT notes for the RE-001 arena snapshot refresh window (node #115).

Window 0x5f07200:
- No PLT calls, no GOT reads, no rip-relative operands and no
  .rodata references in-window. The body is seven instructions:
  endbr64, three load/store qword pairs through rdi-relative
  offsets, and ret. It reads no global state.
- No whole-file qword hit and no R_X86_64_RELATIVE addend resolves
  to the window (see census.py): it is not a vtable slot, not a
  callback and not stored anywhere as data.

Call sites (both direct e8 calls, both with rdi = the outer object):
- 0x5f11b70 in the shard-allocation window 0x5f117a0 (direct refill
  path, after the direct AllocateAligned 0x605d7b0 call and before
  the global and shard lock bytes are cleared).
- 0x5f12083 in the allocation window 0x5f11c40 (direct path, after
  the carved pointer is stored to this + 0x920 and before the
  global lock byte at this + 0x950 is cleared).
