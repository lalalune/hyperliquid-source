# RE-001: the three CompositeEnvWrapper option maps (node #115)

## Question

What are the contents of the three `.bss` `OptionTypeInfo` maps
(`0x6504c60`, `0x6504c20`, `0x6504be0`) that the `CompositeEnvWrapper`
ctor `0x5fd7360` registers with empty names? Merged PR #3950 pinned the
three registrations and recorded the map contents as an open unknown
("not recoverable statically"). This packet resolves it: the static
initializer `0x5fe0760` inserts exactly one entry into each map before
any ctor runs.

## Answer

| Map | Group base (wrapper field) | Entry key | Record pack (type, verification) | Flags |
|-----|---------------------------|-----------|----------------------------------|-------|
| `0x6504c60` | `object+0x48` (Env\*) | `target` | `0x10000001a` (26, 1) | `0x2000` |
| `0x6504c20` | `object+0x28` (FileSystem shared_ptr) | `file_system` | `0x100000016` (22, 1) | `0x400` |
| `0x6504be0` | `object+0x38` (SystemClock shared_ptr) | `clock` | `0x100000016` (22, 1) | `0x400` |

- The pack lives at record `+0xa0` (low dword = OptionType, high dword =
  OptionVerification — the same packing #4320 saw for comparator
  entries); flags live at record `+0xa8`.
- Records are zero-initialized on the stack and no offset store appears
  in any of the three builds, so each entry's field offset is 0: the
  entry addresses its group base itself.
- All three records share the same two `std::function` callback pairs
  (GOT slots `0x64ed8c8`/`0x64ed960` and `0x64ed8d0`/`0x64ed958`),
  whose `_M_invoke` ends are vendor-census-named
  `OptionTypeInfo::AsCustomSharedPtr` handlers (`0x5fd4c30`,
  `0x5fd7470`).
- Each map is referenced binary-wide only by the host ctor
  (RegisterOptions argument) and this initializer (insert argument +
  `__cxa_atexit` argument); the `clock` map's `__cxa_atexit` call reuses
  `r12`, so it has no third `lea`.

## Evidence

- `fn-5fe0760-static-initializer.objdump.txt` — whole-function verbatim
  excerpt of `__static_initialization_and_destruction_0v`
  (`0x5fe0760..0x5fe0fbc`, vendor census 1884/2140 bytes, 88.04%).
- `fn-5d405b0-map-insert-helper.objdump.txt` — whole-function verbatim
  excerpt of the unordered_map lazy-init + insert helper
  (`0x5d405b0..0x5d40f32`; 45 call sites binary-wide; bucket count 1,
  load factor 1.0f, key dedup by length + `memcmp`).
- `maps.tsv`, `literals.tsv`, `frames.txt` — regenerated by `census.py`.
- `records.json` — semantic records only (no per-instruction mnemonic
  lists); generated by `gen_records.py`.
- `verify.py` — self-check: artifact digest, manifest, excerpt
  regeneration, FDEs, raw-binary spot bytes, `.rela.dyn` resolutions,
  lea census, semantic-only records, model tests.

## What this packet does NOT re-pin

The ctor `0x5fd7360`, its base ctor `0x5fceb20` (`rocksdb::Env::Env`),
the destructor, the vtable overrides and `PrepareOptions` are all
pinned by merged PR #3950 and are cited here only.

## Model

`tools/composite_env_maps_115.py` + `tests/test_composite_env_maps_115.py`
(13 tests): the three maps, pack decoding, flags, lookup hit/miss, the
#3950 registration triples matching these maps, callback-pair targets.

## Open unknowns

- The C++ `OptionType` enum names for types 22 and 26 (already open
  from #4239 for types 5 and 23).
- The exact record slots the two callback pairs land in (parse vs
  serialize member mapping).
- The first elements of the two callback pairs (`0x5fcc660`,
  `0x5fd5460`) are not named in the vendor census; recorded as the
  likely `_M_manager` counterparts, not asserted.
- Why `target` carries type 26 / flags `0x2000` while
  `file_system`/`clock` carry type 22 / flags `0x400`.
- The insert helper `0x5d405b0`'s `rdx` argument role (an address inside
  the stack-built key/record pair, compared against key+`0xd8` on the
  already-present path).

the packet's own `verify.py` (artifact digest, manifest, excerpt
