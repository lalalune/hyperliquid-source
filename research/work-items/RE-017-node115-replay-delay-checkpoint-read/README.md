The selected MessagePack restore route calls Exchange map reader `0x2fd7260` from `0x32cb0dc`. This packet follows field 57 through the nested tracker reader to the historical timer word and distinguishes the whole-tracker default from an omitted delay inside a present tracker.

- The outer table at `0x655820` selects `0x2fd925a` for field 57. The branch's diagnostic literal is `advt`. The ordinary field path calls `0x33b2a30` and stages its 0xb8-byte result in the outer frame, beginning at `rsp+0x2e38`.
- The nested table at `0x765648` selects the `adu` field path at `0x33b2d6c` for index 4. The corresponding diagnostic literal is `adu`. The scalar read at `0x33b2d86` is accepted on tag 9, then its word is staged at `rsp+0x3b0`. The record assembly writes it to `rsp+0x348`, relative offset `+0x88` from its `rsp+0x2c0` base. Two 0xb8-byte copies preserve that offset when returning the nested record.
- After parsing, the outer reader checks the first word of its tracker slot. The missing-tracker sentinel takes slot `0x64e27c8` to default constructor `0x3c31160`. Its zeroed XMM register is stored over tracker offsets `+0x80..+0x8f`, including the delay word. The present-tracker path copies the staged record instead.
- Either successful result reaches the copy to historical Exchange offset `+0x2330`, putting the word at `+0x23b8`. [The materialization packet](https://github.com/lalalune/hyperliquid-source/pull/7218) joins this offset to the replay timer. Inside a present tracker, an omitted `adu` calls the named missing-field path at `0x33b3cd3`. That path is separate from the outer tracker constructor.
