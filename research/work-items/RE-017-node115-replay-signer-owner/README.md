# Replay bundle recovered signer and action owner

Target Linux x86-64 node #115, SHA-256 `a6e2897cd051213d004802deca416e8eca62b7a8278fa39f249e98c3867b5301`. This is a selected static ownership/dataflow observation; no captured target instruction was executed or emulated.

The earlier [bundle framing packet #4136](https://github.com/lalalune/hyperliquid-source/pull/4136) establishes the signed span, signer-recovery call and per-span parsing; [#4137](https://github.com/lalalune/hyperliquid-source/pull/4137) establishes inner metadata offsets. This packet follows the selected successful result into the actual replay converter's owned row. It does not establish that this signer is an inner action's authority.

In `0x36abb10`, recovery is called at `0x36abcd5`. A result tag 1 follows the error path. On the selected non-error path, the next 20 bytes of the recovery result are preserved in pieces across the header and inner-span parsing. Each selected successful `0x38d8420` result contributes a `0x168`-byte row to the growing action vector; its existing field semantics are credited to #4137. Once the selected spans are exhausted, the decoder writes the vector descriptor to result +8/+0x10/+0x18, the separately sourced byte-swapped word to +0x20, the 20 recovered bytes to +0x28..+0x3b, and success word `0x1a2` at +0. The unused bytes around these stores are not asserted initialized.

The replay converter `0x4fad300` loads relocation slot `0x64e87f0 -> 0x36abb10` and, for each 40-byte source row, passes the bytes referenced by the row+0x20 object's +0x18/+0x20 to that decoder. On result word `0x1a2`, it copies the 56-byte result payload beginning at +8 into a new `0x48`-byte allocation after two count words initialized to one. It copies the original row's first 32 bytes and replaces its +0x20 pointer with this owner in the output 40-byte row. Thus, on this selected path, the action-vector descriptor occupies owner +0x10/+0x18/+0x20, the separately sourced word occupies +0x28, and the recovered 20-byte value occupies owner +0x30..+0x43. The converter also copies inactive padding bytes; their values are not part of this claim.

The co-location and original allocation/copy are established; successful helper returns, allocation and valid disjoint native storage are premises. This does not prove that the owner reaches admission, assign the optional inner metadata, verify a signature independently, establish authority or authorization, or cover errors, destructors, concurrency and later lifetime. Matching offsets alone do not identify a later consumer.

`verify.py --target /path/to/hl-node` checks the target digest, nine selected instruction windows and the relocation binding, and rejects altered instruction/relocation facts. It is an evidence verifier, not a target execution or a proof of whole-component equivalence. No component gate changes.
