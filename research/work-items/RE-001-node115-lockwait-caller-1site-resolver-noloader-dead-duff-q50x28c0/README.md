## Selector and copy

The copy IS a Duff's-device loop (0x6399fe8..0x639a107), but it is FULLY DEAD. The length is *r8 (`mov (%r14),%rdx` at 0x6399f71, r14 = entry r8); if the length is exactly 8, the qword from the stack temp (%rsp) is stored whole to *rcx (`mov %rsi,(%r12)` at 0x6399f7a) and the window exits 0 (`xor %ebp,%ebp` at 0x6399f7e). A null dest or null length pointer also exits 0 (`je 0x6399f7e` at 0x6399f6a / 0x6399f6f). Otherwise n = min(len,8) (`mov $0x8,%edi` at 0x6399fa0, `mov %rsp,%r9` at 0x6399fa5, `cmp %rdi,%rdx` at 0x6399fa8, `cmova %rdi,%rdx` at 0x6399fab); len < 8 goes to the small tail (`jb 0x639a110` at 0x6399fb2), which copies n = len bytes by 4/2/1-byte decomposition (0x639a110..0x639a159) and exits 22 with *r8 = n. The 1-byte path sets ebp = first byte (`movzbl (%rsp),%ebp` at 0x639a119) as a transient, but that def is dead — the writeback's `mov $0x16,%ebp` at 0x639a129 overwrites it on both paths out (fall-through to 0x639a126, or via the 2-byte path at 0x639a14a then `jmp 0x639a126` at 0x639a159). The 4-byte path (`mov (%rsp),%r8d` at 0x639a133, `mov %edx,%r10d` at 0x639a137) and the 2-byte path (`mov %edx,%r13d` at 0x639a14a) set no ebp def at all — as in #401/#402, there is no `mov %edx,%ebp` anywhere in this window (unlike #373–#400). Len > 8 reaches the n == 8 path: `mov %edx,%eax` at 0x6399fb8 sets eax = 8; the first qword is stored to dest+0 (`mov %rsi,(%r12)` at 0x6399fba), `lea 0x8(%r12),%rdi` at 0x6399fbe, re-loaded from src+0 (`mov -0x8(%r9,%rax,1),%rcx` at 0x6399fc3 — with eax == 8 reads src+0, not src+8), `and $~7,%rdi` at 0x6399fc8, and stored AGAIN to dest+0 (`mov %rcx,-0x8(%r12,%rax,1)` at 0x6399fcc), then r12d = (dest - ((dest+8)&~7) + 8) & ~7 = 0 (`sub %rdi,%r12` at 0x6399fd1, `sub %r12,%r9` at 0x6399fd4, `add %edx,%r12d` at 0x6399fd7, `and $~7,%r12d` at 0x6399fda), so `cmp $0x8,%r12d` at 0x6399fde leaves r12d < 8 and the loop-entry jb (`jb 0x639a126` at 0x6399fe2) is ALWAYS taken to the writeback. The dispatch tail (0x6399fe8..0x639a0a1, `shr $0x3,%esi` at 0x6399ff7 + `and $0x7,%esi` at 0x6399ffe, seven je tail entries for esi 0..6) and the 8-at-a-time body (0x639a0a1..0x639a107, back-edge `jb 0x639a0a1` at 0x639a107) are never entered — the loop's only CFG entry is the fall-through from 0x6399fe2 (no jump in the window targets 0x6399fe8; the body entry 0x639a0a1 is reached only from `je 0x639a0a1` at 0x639a00c, the fall-through from 0x639a09b, and the back-edge at 0x639a107, all inside the dead region).
