# Proof boundary
Observe the retained native certificate and complete Capstone exports first.
The normal path reads three bytes, passes both all-u8 gates, advances cursor
and counter by3 and stores the defined fields before jumping to the epilogue.
The two unspecified incoming-XMM payloads are deliberately excluded.
For n>0, let e=floor(log2(n)); (1023+e)<<52 plus
(n-2**e)<<(52-e) exactly encodes n; n=0 encodes positive zero.
No integer exceeds255, hence all conversions are exact under every rounding
mode. Three channels are independent. The original static certificate remains
the observation source; freshly authored Rust is checked against its literal
operands, not against itself. Corruption witnesses attack each result class.
The deterministic corpus predates this publication but is not an independent
public pre-implementation freeze. No stronger chronology claim is made.
